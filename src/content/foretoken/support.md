## Getting started

**Do I need an account?**
No sign-in is needed. Foretoken creates a guest account when you first open it, and you can start your
free trial from it. Your follows, saves, alert settings and subscription are kept in that guest account.
Sign in with Apple or with an email code if you want to keep them across devices or after reinstalling. Unused guest accounts are deleted after 90 days.

**How do I sign in?**
Open Profile and tap **Sign in to keep your saves**, then choose Sign in with Apple or Email code.
Everything you did as a guest moves into your account.

## Alerts

**How many alerts will I get?**
Usually 3 to 5 a day. You choose a daily limit of 3, 4 or 5 in Alert settings. A small number of
break-through stories can go past your limit, and we never send more than 7 pushes in a day. Quiet hours
(overnight by default) pause alerts; those stories wait for you in the feed.

**Which alerts can I choose?**
With Premium (or during your free trial) you can turn on any alert type, plus company and keyword alerts.
Without a subscription, Foretoken sends no alerts.

**I am not getting alerts.**
Check iOS Settings > Notifications > Foretoken, then Profile > Alert settings in the app. No alerts are sent
during your quiet hours.

## Forecasts

**Where do the forecasts come from?**
From Manifold, a play-money community forecasting site. Foretoken shows them as percentages, the same way
in every region.

**Can I take part in a forecast from Foretoken?**
No. Forecasts are display-only context from a third party, not predictions from this app and not
financial advice. You cannot stake anything in Foretoken, and it has no links to forecast or market
pages.

## Plans and trials

Foretoken needs a Foretoken Premium subscription after a free trial: 14 days on Annual, 7 days on Monthly,
for eligible new subscribers. We remind you 2 days before the trial ends. Without a subscription you see
a preview of the top headlines.

**Redeem a code**
Open Profile and tap **Redeem a code** to use an App Store offer code.

**Restore purchases**
If you bought Premium and it is not showing (for example after reinstalling or on a new iPhone), open
Profile and tap **Restore purchases**, or tap **Restore purchases** on the Premium screen. Use the same
Apple Account you bought it with. If Premium says it is linked to another Foretoken account, choose
**Move it here** to bring it to the account you are using.

**Cancel Premium**
Open iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Foretoken Premium > Cancel Subscription. Cancel at
least 24 hours before the end of the trial or the current period to avoid the next charge. Premium stays
on until the period ends.

**Refunds**
Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request a refund at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

## Your data

**Delete your account**
1. Open Profile.
2. Tap **Delete account** (or **Delete my data** if you never signed in).
3. Confirm.

This removes your saves, follows, alert settings, notification history, reading history, interests and
sign-in. It cannot be undone.
**Deleting your account does not cancel Premium**: cancel it in Settings first if you do not want to be
charged again.

You can also email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you sign in with (or with your Support ID) and
we will delete your data within 30 days.

**Reset my interests**
Profile > **Reset my interests** clears what Foretoken learned about your reading; your feed starts again
from the companies you follow. In the Live tab's **For you** list, a story's **More options** menu has
**Why am I seeing this?** and **Show fewer like this**; the **Latest** order shows every story in time
order.

**Get a copy of your data, or correct it**
Email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We answer within 30 days. Our Privacy Policy explains what we keep:
https://vakastudio.com/foretoken/privacy.

## Report a problem with a story

Open the story, tap the More menu, then **Report a problem**, choose a reason and add a short note if you like. We review
every report and fix summaries that are wrong.
