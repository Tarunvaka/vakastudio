Vaka Studio is a small, independent studio making calm iPhone apps that do one thing well.

Every app starts with an everyday question, like “when did I last get a haircut?”, and ends with the simplest honest answer we can build. No accounts unless they’re needed. No tracking. No tricks. Just useful things, made with care, that you can keep for years.

How we build

Four promises,
in every app.

01

Small on purpose

One job, done well. If a feature needs a manual, it doesn’t ship.

02

Private by default

We collect as little as possible. Usually nothing at all.

03

Native and calm

Built with Apple’s own tools, so every app feels at home on your iPhone.

04

Made to last

No ads, no dark patterns, nothing designed to keep you scrolling.
